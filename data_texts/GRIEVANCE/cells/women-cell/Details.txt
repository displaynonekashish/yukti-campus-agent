### About Women Cell

The Women Cell at Engineering College Ajmer is dedicated to safeguarding the rights of all female members of the institution and addressing any issues related to gender discrimination or harassment. The cell works to create a safe, supportive, and empowering environment for women students and staff.

### Primary Roles and Objectives

The primary roles and objectives of the Women's Cell are:
- To safeguard the rights of all female members of the institution
- To address any issues related to gender discrimination or harassment
- To promote gender equality and women's empowerment
- To ensure a safe and secure campus environment for women

### Grievance Redressal

The cell serves as a confidential platform for female students and staff to voice complaints or concerns about harassment, discrimination, or any other personal challenges. 

**Key Functions:**
- Provides confidential platform for reporting complaints
- Investigates complaints thoroughly
- Recommends appropriate action
- Ensures fair and timely resolution of grievances
- Maintains confidentiality throughout the process

**How to File a Complaint:**
- Students and staff can directly approach the members or convener of the women's cell with their concerns
- Anonymous suggestion/complaint boxes are available in various places on campus for those who are hesitant to come forward directly
- For complaints, you can contact the members of the women cell

### Awareness and Sensitization

The Women Cell organizes various programs to raise awareness and promote gender equality:

**Activities Include:**
- Workshops on gender equality and women's rights
- Seminars on laws related to women
- Campaigns on health and hygiene
- Awareness programs on preventing harassment and discrimination
- Educational sessions on women's legal rights and protections

### Skill Development and Empowerment

The cell aims to enhance self-esteem and self-confidence by providing opportunities for skill development:

**Programs Offered:**
- Self-defense training
- Vocational courses
- Entrepreneurial activities
- Career guidance and counseling
- Leadership development programs

**Goal:** To help women become self-reliant and achieve their academic and career goals.

### Counselling and Support

The Women Cell offers comprehensive support services:

**Services Include:**
- Emotional support for those facing difficulties
- Professional counseling services
- Guidance for academic challenges
- Support for psychological or emotional difficulties
- Referral to specialized services when needed

### Promoting a Safe Environment

The cell works with college administration to implement policies and practices that ensure the campus is a safe place for women:

**Initiatives:**
- Implementation of anti-harassment policies
- Regular safety audits and assessments
- Collaboration with security and administration
- Creating awareness about safety measures
- Ensuring zero tolerance for any form of violence or discrimination

### How to Access the Women's Cell

**Direct Approach:**
- Students and staff can directly approach the members or convener of the women's cell with their concerns
- Office hours and contact information available at the Women Cell office

**Complaint Boxes:**
- Anonymous suggestion/complaint boxes are placed in various locations on campus
- Available for those who prefer to report concerns anonymously
- Checked regularly by Women Cell members

**Contact Members:**
- For complaints or concerns, you can contact the members of the women cell directly
- Members are available during office hours and can be reached through the college administration

### Emergency Helpline

For immediate support on serious issues, the following helpline is available:

**NCW 24x7 Women Helpline:** 7827170170

This is a 24/7 helpline provided by the National Commission for Women for immediate support and assistance.

### Important Information

- All complaints and concerns are handled with strict confidentiality
- The Women Cell ensures fair and impartial investigation of all complaints
- Support is available for all female students and staff members
- The cell works in coordination with college administration and other support services
- Regular awareness programs are conducted throughout the academic year

### Contact

For Women Cell related queries, complaints, or support:
- Visit the Women Cell office during office hours
- Contact the Women Cell members or convener
- Use the anonymous complaint boxes available on campus
- For emergencies, use the NCW 24x7 Women Helpline: 7827170170
