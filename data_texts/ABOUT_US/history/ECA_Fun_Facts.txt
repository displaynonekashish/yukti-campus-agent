*** ECA AJMER: 25+ FACTS YOU SHOULD KNOW ***

### 🏛️ HISTORY & ORIGINS (The "Did You Know?")
1. **The "Mahila" Origin:** ECA Ajmer was originally established in **1997** as the "Mahila Engineering College" (exclusively for women). It was the first of its kind in the state before eventually becoming a co-ed Government Engineering College.
2. **The Affiliation Journey:** The college has been under three different universities! It started under **MDSU** (Ajmer), moved to **RTU** (Kota), and is currently affiliated with **BTU** (Bikaner Technical University).
3. **Oldest in the Pack:** Established in 1997, it is one of the oldest engineering colleges in the Rajasthan government sector, senior to many peers.
4. **TEQIP-III Status:** ECA is a World Bank-funded **TEQIP-III Institute**. This grant is the reason behind the modernized labs and high-end equipment in the Mechanical and CS departments.

### 🏆 CAMPUS FLEX (Achievements)
5. **AICTE Award:** Our own faculty, **Dr. Jyoti Gajrani**, received the **AICTE Visvesvaraya Best Teacher Award (2021)**, a massive national honor.
6. **The "Param" Supercomputer:** We have a lab named "Param Lab" (G-24), which is dedicated to high-performance computing, a rarity in state colleges.
7. **NTA Exam Hub:** Due to its massive computer infrastructure (Aryabhata & Ramanujan Labs), ECA is a designated center for national exams like JEE Mains and GATE.
8. **NPTEL Leaders:** The college has a very active NPTEL Local Chapter, with students consistently ranking in the "Gold" and "Elite" categories in national MOOC exams.
9. **Gate Factory:** The Mechanical and CS departments are known as "PSU Factories" because of the high number of students clearing GATE and joining companies like ONGC and BHEL every year.

### 📍 LOCATION & VIBE
10. **The "Hilltop" Exercise:** The college is built on the Aravali foothills. Walking from the main highway gate to the New Building is legitimately considered a cardio workout by students.
11. **Nareli View:** The college creates a perfect frame for the **Nareli Jain Temple** across the highway. The sunset view from the New Building corridors is the best photo op on campus.
12. **Peaceful Isolation:** Being at "Badliya Chouraha" means zero city traffic noise, but it also means you have to plan your snacks because the city is 10km away!
13. **Peacock Territory:** It is very common to spot Peacocks dancing on the roof of the Old Building or near the Girls' Hostel, especially during the monsoon.

### 🤖 TECH & LABS
19. **RCAT on the Ruins:** The high-tech **RCAT (Robotics Center)** stands exactly where the nostalgic "Old Canteen" used to be. Out with the samosas, in with the robots!
20. **Naming Tradition:** Notice that CS Labs are named after scientists (Ramanujan, Aryabhata, Codd) while other departments often use functional names (Heat Transfer Lab).
21. **Wi-Fi Zones:** The campus is Wi-Fi enabled, but specific logins (Cyberoam/Fortinet) are usually required, which are managed by the Computer Center.

### 🎭 CLUBS & CULTURE
22. **Tarangini's Legacy:** Tarangini isn't just a fest; it's one of the oldest cultural fests in the region, running for over two decades.
23. **SAC is HQ:** The **Student Activity Center (SAC)** is the headquarters for all fun. If you want to join a club, that's the building to visit.
24. **NCC & NSS:** The college has very active NCC and NSS wings that lead the Independence Day parades and campus cleanliness drives.
25. **Alumni Network:** We have a strong alumni base in the US and Europe, often coming back for "Alumni Meets" organized in December/January.
