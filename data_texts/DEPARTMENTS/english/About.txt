The Department of English at Government Engineering College Ajmer plays a pivotal role in nurturing the communication and soft skills of engineering students, developing students' language competency in listening, speaking, reading and writing, ensuring they are industry-ready and globally competent. • Our ### Vision & Mission is to enhance students’ proficiency in English through innovative and learner-centered pedagogy, equipping them to meet academic, professional, and societal challenges. • Laboratories and Facilities Language Laboratory with interactive software to enhance listening, speaking, reading, and writing (LSRW) skills. • Smart Class rooms equipped for audio-visual learning and presentations.
