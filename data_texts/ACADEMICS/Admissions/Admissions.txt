### Engineering College Ajmer (ECA) - Admission Information

### B.Tech Admission Process

**Primary Route: REAP (Rajasthan Engineering Admission Process)**

**Counseling Authority:** Centre for Electronic Governance (CEG), Jaipur

**Basis of Selection:**
- **Priority 1:** JEE Main Score (Rank) - REAP General Merit Rank is calculated primarily based on JEE Main AIR (All India Rank)
- **Priority 2:** Class 12th Percentile (if seats remain vacant after exhausting JEE Main candidates)

**Eligibility Criteria:**
- **Educational:** Passed 10+2 examination with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects along with one optional subject (Chemistry/Bio-Tech/Biology/Technical Vocational)
- **Minimum Marks:** At least 45% aggregate (40% for SC/ST/Non-creamy layer OBC/MBC of Rajasthan)

**Key Documents Required:**
- JEE Main Scorecard
- Class 10th & 12th Marksheets
- Domicile Certificate
- Category Certificate
- Medical Fitness Certificate

**Important Notes:**
- Admissions are centralized and conducted by CEG, Rajasthan
- There is no separate college-level entrance exam
- Computer Science and IT seats fill very fast (usually in Round 1 & 2)
- Core branches like Civil/Mechanical often have seats available in Spot Rounds (direct college-level admission) for candidates with lower percentages

**Tentative Cutoffs (REAP General Merit Rank):**
Note: These are estimated State Merit Ranks, not JEE Main All India Ranks. A REAP Rank of 2000 roughly corresponds to a much higher JEE Main AIR (e.g., 60k–90k range) depending on the applicant pool.

| Course Branch | Tentative Closing Rank (REAP Merit) | Likely JEE Main Percentile Required |
|---------------|-------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Computer Science (CSE) | 1,200 – 2,500 | ~90 - 93 %ile |
| Information Technology (IT) | 2,500 – 6,000 | ~85 - 89 %ile |
| Electronics & Comm. (ECE) | 5,000 – 9,000 | ~80 - 85 %ile |
| Electrical Engineering | 10,000 – 14,000 | ~70 - 75 %ile |
| Mechanical Engineering | 12,000 – 18,000+ | ~60 - 70 %ile |
| Civil Engineering | 15,000 – 20,000+ | ~50 - 65 %ile |
| EIC (Instrumentation) | 20,000+ | ~50 - 60 %ile |

**Lateral Entry (LEEP):**
- Direct admission to 2nd year B.Tech is available for Diploma/B.Sc. holders via LEEP counseling
- Process: LEEP (Lateral Entry Engineering Program) counseling

**B.Tech Programs Offered:**
1. Civil Engineering (Intake: 60)
2. Computer Engineering (Intake: 120)
3. Computer Science & Engineering - Cyber Security (Intake: 60)
4. Electrical Engineering (Intake: 60)
5. Electronics & Communication Engineering (Intake: 90)
6. Electronics Instrumentation & Control Engineering (Intake: 60)

### M.Tech Admission Process

**Primary Route: CAM (Centralized Admission for M.Tech)**

**Admissions are coordinated centrally for Rajasthan technical universities.**

**Basis of Selection:**
- **Priority 1:** Valid GATE Score (Scholarship available for GATE qualified candidates - approx. ₹12,400/month stipend)
- **Priority 2:** Merit in Qualifying Degree (B.E./B.Tech percentage) for non-GATE candidates

**Eligibility:**
- B.E./B.Tech in relevant discipline with 50% marks (45% for SC/ST/OBC)

**Tentative Cutoff:**
- **GATE Candidates:** Valid Scorecard (Qualifying marks ~300-350 score is usually safe for admission)
- **Non-GATE:** Open admission in most branches (except CS/Production) due to seat availability

**Specializations Offered:**
1. Computer Science & Engineering (Intake: 9)
2. Software Engineering (Intake: 9)
3. Digital Communication (Intake: 9)
4. Power Systems (Intake: 18)
5. Production Engineering (Intake: 18)
6. Renewable Energy (Intake: 18)
7. VLSI Design & Embedded Systems (Intake: 18)
8. Information Technology (Intake: 9)

### MCA Admission Process

**Primary Route: RMCAAP (Rajasthan MCA Admission Process)**

**Exams Accepted:**
- **Priority:** NIMCET (Preferred)
- **Alternative:** Direct admission based on Graduation percentage if seats remain vacant after NIMCET counseling

**Eligibility:**
- Bachelor's Degree (min 50%) + Mathematics at 12th or Graduation level
- BCA/B.Sc. (Computer Science)/B.Sc. (IT)/B.E. (CSE)/B.Tech (CSE)/B.E. (IT)/B.Tech (IT) or equivalent Degree
- OR Passed any graduation degree (B.E./B.Tech/B.Sc/B.Com/B.A./B.Voc. etc.) preferably with Mathematics at 10+2 level or at Graduation level
- Minimum 50% marks (45% marks for reserved category)

**Cutoff Trend:**
- **NIMCET:** Any valid rank usually secures a seat
- **Direct Merit:** Since many seats often remain vacant after NIMCET counseling, admission is frequently given based on Graduation % (B.Sc./BCA with Math)

**Intake:** 60 seats

**Course Duration:** Two Years

---

### MBA Admission Process

**Primary Route: RMACP (Rajasthan Management Admission Process)**

**Exams Accepted:**
- **Primary:** CMAT (Preferred)
- **Alternative:** RMAT and other approved tests
- **Direct Admission:** Available for vacant seats based on Graduation % after the counseling rounds

**Eligibility:**
- Bachelor's Degree in any discipline (min 50%) from a recognized university

**Specializations:**
- Finance
- Marketing
- HRM (Human Resource Management)
- Operations

**Cutoff Trend:**
- **CMAT:** ~25+ Percentile is often sufficient for admission
- **Direct Admission:** Available for vacant seats based on Graduation % after counseling rounds

**Intake:** 60 seats

### Important Notes

1. **Admission Process:** All admissions are conducted through centralized counseling processes (REAP, CAM, RMCAAP, RMACP). There is no separate college-level entrance exam.

2. **Spot Rounds:** If seats remain vacant after counseling rounds, direct college-level admission (Spot Rounds) may be available for eligible candidates.

3. **Document Verification:** All original documents must be produced at the time of admission for verification.

4. **Scholarships:** GATE qualified M.Tech students are eligible for scholarship (approx. ₹12,400/month stipend).

5. **Updates:** Admission dates, cutoffs, and procedures may change annually. Always refer to the official website or contact the admission office for the most current information.
