Engineering College Ajmer ​ ▼ Hindi Academic Section Details Circulars Members Details The Academic Section of Engineering College, Ajmer is responsible for the planning, coordination, and monitoring of all academic activities within the institution. It acts as the central administrative unit for both Undergraduate (U. G. ) and Postgraduate (P. G. ) programs, ensuring that all teaching-learning processes are conducted as per the guidelines of Bikaner Technical University (BTU), Bikaner and other regulatory bodies. The section oversees the preparation and execution of the Academic Calendar, curriculum implementation, examination-related coordination, and maintenance of academic records. It also facilitates inter- departmental communication and supports the introduction of new programs and courses that align with emerging fields of science, technology, and industry relevance. With a commitment to academic excellence, transparency, and continuous improvement, the Academic Section ensures smooth academic functioning and quality assurance across all departments. The Academic Section functions under the super vision of the Academic Coordinator, supported by the U. G. Coordinator and P. G. Coordinator. It works in close coordination with the Heads of Departments, Examination Section, and Registrar Office to ensure the timely and effective implementation of all academic activities at both U. G. and P. G. levels. Vission “To achieve and sustain academic excellence through effective planning, coordination, and continuous improvement in teaching-learning processes, fostering innovation, and promoting the holistic development of students in alignment with institutional and university goals. ” Mission To plan and implement structured academic activities in accordance with institutional and university norms. To coordinate and monitor the teaching-learning process across all departments for effective curriculum delivery. To promote the development of new programs that address emerging technologies and societal needs. To maintain transparency, accountability, and efficiency in all academic operations. To enhance coordination among departments for achieving institutional objectives and academic quality benchmarks. Objectives The Academic Section is dedicated to ensuring efficient academic administration and excellence at both U. G. and P. G. levels. Its key objectives are: To prepare, update, and monitor the Academic Calendar for all programs in alignment with BTU guidelines. To coordinate academic schedules, time tables, and curriculum implementation across departments. To process proposals for the introduction of new U. G. and P. G. programs and courses in emerging disciplines. To maintain accurate academic records, correspondence, and departmental reports. To ensure timely execution of academic policies, examinations, and quality standards. Academic Calender (Bikaner Technical University, Bikaner)
