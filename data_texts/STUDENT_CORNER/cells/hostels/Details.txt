### About Hostels

The institute has a girls hostel in the premises which was constructed under the AICTE scheme for SC/ST students. The hostel became functional in the year 2019. It has 20 triple seater rooms with a total capacity of 60 students.

### Facilities

The hostel provides the following facilities:
- Mess (dining facility)
- Common Room
- Warden Room
- Visitors Room
- Guest Room
- Kitchen & Toilet facilities
- Each room has: cot, table, chair, ceiling fan with regulator, and light fitting

### Room Allotment

- Accommodation in the Hostel cannot be claimed as a matter of right
- Allotment is based on merit and eligibility as decided by the Allotment Committee
- Rooms are allotted for one academic year/two semesters
- Students must occupy the room allotted to them and cannot exchange rooms without written permission
- Students must deposit one set of room keys in the hostel office
- Students must use their own lock and key for their room

### Mess Rules

- All hostel residents are automatically members of the Hostel Mess
- Hostels are meant for both boarding and lodging - cannot use for boarding or lodging only
- Food will not be served in rooms - must dine in the dining hall
- Day scholars cannot be entertained as guests in the mess
- Wasting food is prohibited - first offence: warning, second offence: fine of Rs. 500/-, repeated offences: strict disciplinary action
- Mess reduction is admissible only for: approved study holidays, vacations, periods recommended by HOD for sports/competitions/seminars, and periods for interviews/training with TPO/HOD recommendation
- If number of students is less than 10% of total strength, mess may be closed with 24 hours notice

### Timings and Outing Rules

**Outing Timings (Sundays and Holidays only):**
- Summers (1st April to 30th September): 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM
- Winters (1st October to 31st March): 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM
- Students must fill movement register before going out and while coming in

**Important Rules:**
- Hostel residents cannot leave campus without prior permission from Warden/Chief Warden
- Students are not allowed to go out during class hours
- Students staying out beyond specified time will face disciplinary action
- For going home during vacations, application must be submitted at least 2 days in advance with parent consent via fax/email/whatsapp
- Once student reaches home, parents must inform hostel authorities

### Visitors

- Visiting hours: 4 PM to 6 PM only
- Only parents/guardians are allowed to enter hostel, and only up to visitors' room
- All visitors must make entries in Visitors' Book at hostel entrance
- Male students are not allowed to visit girls hostel
- Parents/guardians are not allowed inside student rooms
- Parents must give undertaking to cooperate with hostel authorities

### Guest Accommodation

- If mother of hosteller needs accommodation (1-2 days only), must intimate hostel office 7 days before
- Accommodation subject to availability of Guest Room
- Guest charges apply as per rates fixed by Hostel Management
- Day scholars and other relatives are not permitted to stay as guests
- Entertaining unauthorized guests: fine of Rs. 2000/- and expulsion from hostel
- No guest permitted to stay overnight in hosteller's room - violation: fine of Rs. 2500/- and expulsion

### Prohibited Items and Activities

**Strictly Prohibited:**
- Electrical appliances: electric stove, heaters, iron, refrigerator, infrared cooker
- Private cooking in rooms or anywhere in hostel
- Personal TV, VCR, VCD/DVD players
- Audio systems causing inconvenience
- Smoking, alcoholic drinks, drugs, gambling
- Firearms, lethal weapons, poisonous things
- Obscene literature/video films
- Pet animals
- Mopeds, motorcycles, scooters, cars (without permission)
- Private practice/job/tuition
- Celebrating parties (like birthday) inside hostel
- Using hostel terrace
- Formation of associations based on regions, caste or creed

**Silent Hours:**
- Weekdays: 10 PM to 8 AM
- Weekends: 11 PM to 8 AM
- TV and music systems should not be used during silent hours

### Ragging

- Ragging in any form is banned and is a cognizable offence
- Severe punishment including expulsion from Hostel and Institute
- Any incident of ragging must be reported immediately to Hostel Authorities
- Non-reporting by victim or witness is also considered an offence
- All hostellers must sign Anti-Ragging Undertaking

### Room Maintenance and Cleanliness

- Students are responsible for cleanliness and maintenance of their room
- Rooms, common areas, and surroundings must be kept clean and hygienic
- Toilets and bathrooms must be kept clean - collective fine if not maintained
- Notices must not be pasted on walls, walls must not be scribbled on
- Furniture and fixtures must not be moved from allotted place
- Any damage to property will result in monetary penalty equal to market price plus fine of Rs. 1000/- per item
- Rooms are subject to inspection by Institute/Hostel authorities

### Vacating Hostel

- Students must vacate hostel within 2 days of completion of examination
- Before vacating, must fill 'Room Vacating Slip' in triplicate and submit to Warden
- All furniture and fixtures must be handed over in proper condition
- Any damage will result in compensation recovery
- Students cannot vacate hostel in middle of session except on genuine grounds approved by Principal
- For mid-session vacation: must notify 1 month in advance, deposit/caution money forfeited, fees NOT refunded except mess charges on pro-rata basis

### Disciplinary Measures

- Violation of rules will result in disciplinary action by Hostel Administration
- Depending on gravity, cases may be forwarded to Institute Disciplinary Committee/Proctor Section
- Hostel Administration reserves right to terminate occupancy for willful disobedience, frequent violation of rules, damage to property, or anti-national activities
- In such cases: deposit/caution money forfeited, fees NOT refunded except mess charges on pro-rata basis

### Grievance Redressal

- Hostellers can approach Hostel Administration in writing for help, guidance, or redressal of grievances
- For disputes not satisfactorily resolved, appeal can be made to Principal through Warden/Chief Warden
- Decision of Principal is final and binding

### Items to Bring at Admission

Students must bring: Bed sheets, Mattress, Curtains, Quilt for winter, Bed linen, Pillow, Pillow covers, Blanket, Bucket and plastic mug, Toiletries, Emergency light, First aid box and personal medicines, Lock and keys, Passport size photos (student and visitors), Stationery items, Registration form (from college website), Hostel undertaking signed by student and parents (from college website)

### Important Notes

- Students residing in hostel stay at their own risk, liability and consequences
- Hostel authorities will not be responsible for loss of personal property due to theft, fire, or any other cause
- Students must inform hostel authorities immediately if their eligibility status changes
- Students removed from college will automatically cease to be hostel members
- Hostel rules and regulations may be amended from time to time - inmates will be informed through circulars
