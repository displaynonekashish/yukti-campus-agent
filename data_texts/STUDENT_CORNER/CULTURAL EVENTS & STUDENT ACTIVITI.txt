*** CULTURAL EVENTS & STUDENT ACTIVITIES (SAC) ***

### 1. MAJOR ANNUAL FESTS

**Event Name: TARANGINI (The Annual Cultural Fest)**
* **Description:** Tarangini is the flagship cultural festival of Government Engineering College Ajmer. It is the most anticipated event of the year where students showcase their artistic talents.
* **Duration:** Usually spans 2-3 days.
* **Key Competitions:**
    * **Dance:** Solo Dance, Group Dance, Folk Dance (Ghoomar/Kalbelia), and Street Battle.
    * **Music:** Solo Singing (Classical/Western), Battle of Bands, and Instrumental face-offs.
    * **Drama:** Nukkad Natak (Street Play), Skits, and Mono-acting.
    * **Fashion:** "Panache" - The Fashion Show (Theme-based ramp walk).
    * **Star Night:** The closing ceremony usually featuring a DJ Night or a guest performance.
* **Organizer:** Student Activity Center (SAC) and the Cultural Club.

**Event Name: TECHNOTSAV (The Annual Technical Fest)**
* **Description:** Technotsav is the technical counterpart to Tarangini, focusing on engineering skills, innovation, and coding.
* **Key Competitions:**
    * **Robotics:** Robo War, Line Follower, Robo Soccer.
    * **Coding:** Hackathons, Code-Debug, Blind Coding.
    * **Civil/Mech:** Bridge Building, CAD Design, Junk-Yard Wars.
    * **Gaming:** LAN Gaming (CS, Valorant, BGMI) tournaments.
    * **Exhibitions:** Project displays and Model presentations.

### 2. CLUBS & SOCIETIES (Under SAC)

**Student Activity Center (SAC)**
* **Role:** SAC is the central body that manages all extra-curricular activities, fests, and clubs in the college.
* **Leadership:** Headed by a Chief Coordinator (Faculty) and Student Coordinators.

**Creative Art Society (CAS)**
* **Focus:** Painting, Sketching, Rangoli making, and decoration during college events.
* **Activities:** They organize art exhibitions during Tarangini and decorate the campus for Independence Day and Republic Day.

**Music & Dance Club**
* **Focus:** Training and rehearsing for college functions.
* **Activities:** Flash mobs, jam sessions, and preparing performances for AICTE events.

**Literary Club**
* **Focus:** Debates, Poetry (Kavi Sammelan), Elocution, and Quizzes.
* **Activities:** They host the "Hindi Diwas" competitions and English debate championships.

**Photography Club**
* **Focus:** Capturing moments during college events.
* **Activities:** Photo-walks, photography exhibitions, and managing the media coverage for Tarangini/Technotsav.

### 3. TRADITIONAL & DEPARTMENTAL EVENTS

**National Days (Independence & Republic Day)**
* **Details:** Mandatory flag hoisting at the administrative block.
* **Activities:** Parade by NCC cadets, patriotic songs by the Music Club, and sweet distribution.

**Dandiya Night**
* **Description:** Celebrated during Navratri. Students and faculty dress in traditional attire (Garba look) for a night of Garba and Dandiya Raas on the college grounds or basketball court.

**Fresher's Party (Abhinandan)**
* **Description:** Welcoming event for 1st-year students organized by 2nd-year students.
* **Highlight:** Mr. and Ms. Fresher competition.

**Farewell Party**
* **Description:** Send-off event for Final Year students organized by 3rd-year students.
* **Highlight:** Title distribution and emotional speeches.

**Engineers Day (September 15)**
* **Description:** Celebrated in honor of Sir M. Visvesvaraya. Departments organize technical quizzes and expert lectures on this day.

### 4. IMPORTANT COORDINATION POINTS

* **How to Participate:** Students must register with the respective Club Coordinators at the SAC office (usually located near the Main Stage or Old Building).
* **Selection Process:** Auditions are held approx. 1 month before Tarangini for Dance, Music, and Drama events.
* **Certificates:** Participation certificates are awarded for all major events, which are useful for the "Extra-Curricular" section of the resume.
