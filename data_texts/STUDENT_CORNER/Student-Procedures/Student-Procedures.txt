### Engineering College Ajmer - Student Procedures & Guidelines

### 1. Applying for Medical Leave

**When to use:** If you are sick and miss classes/labs.

**The Process:**

**Notification:**
- Inform your Proctor or HOD via email/phone immediately if possible

**Documents Needed:**
1. Formal Application addressed to the Principal (via HOD)
2. Medical Certificate (Original) from a Govt. Doctor or registered practitioner
3. Fitness Certificate (Must be submitted when you return to college)

**Submission:**
- Submit these to your Department Office immediately upon joining back

**Pro Tip:** Keep a photocopy of your medical documents. If your attendance falls short at the end of the semester, this approved leave is your only defense against detention.

---

### 2. Internship & NOC Letters

**When to use:** Summer training (after 4th/6th sem) or Semester-long internships.

**The Process:**

**Step 1: Offer Letter**
- First, get a confirmation/offer letter from the company where you want to intern

**Step 2: Application**
- Write an application to the Training & Placement (T&P) Officer (Head TPO) requesting an NOC (No Objection Certificate)

**Step 3: Routing**
- Take the application to your HOD first for a signature/remarks (checking if academic dates clash)
- Submit it to the T&P Cell (Main Building)

**Step 4: Collection**
- Collect the signed NOC from the T&P Cell after 1-2 days

**Key Requirement:** Company Offer Letter

---

### 3. Getting a Transfer Certificate (TC) & Caution Money

**When to use:** After completing your degree or if you are leaving the college mid-course.

**The "No Dues" Struggle:**
- You cannot get your TC or Caution Money (approx. ₹7,500 usually) without a "No Dues Certificate."

**Steps to Clear Dues:**

**Step 1: Get the Form**
- Collect the "No Dues Form" from the Proctor Section or download it from the college website

**Step 2: The Run-Around - Get Signatures From:**
- **Library:** Return all books
- **Sports Dept:** Ensure no equipment is pending
- **Hostel Warden:** Even if you were a day scholar, you need a "Nil" signature
- **Labs/Department:** Return components/files
- **Account Section:** Verify all semester fees are paid

**Step 3: Submission**
- Submit the fully signed form + Original Caution Money Receipt (safeguard this!) + Bank Passbook copy (for refund) to the Accounts Section

**Important:** Keep your Original Caution Money Receipt safe - you will need it for refund!

---

### 4. Scholarships (Social Justice / Samaj Kalyan)

**Platform:**
- Applications are usually done via the SJMS Portal (Social Justice & Empowerment Dept, Rajasthan) or National Scholarship Portal (NSP)

**College Verification:**
- After applying online, you must submit the hard copy of the application + Income Certificate + Caste Certificate + Fee Receipt to the Scholarship Section (usually in the Proctor Office or Admin Block)

**Warning:** If you don't submit the hard copy, the college will not verify your form online, and your scholarship will be rejected.

**Required Documents:**
- Hard copy of online application
- Income Certificate
- Caste Certificate
- Fee Receipt

---

### 5. Migration & Degree Certificate (Crucial)

**Know Your University:**

**Old Batches (Pre-2018 approx):**
- You are likely under RTU (Rajasthan Technical University, Kota)
- You must apply for Migration/Degree at the RTU website/office

**New Batches (2018 onwards):**
- You are likely under BTU (Bikaner Technical University)
- You must apply for Migration/Degree at the BTU website/office

**The Process:**
1. Apply online on the respective University website (RTU or BTU)
2. The college (ECA) only forwards your request; the certificate comes from the University
3. Follow the university's procedure for certificate collection

---

### 6. Bonafide / Character Certificate

**Simple Process:**
- Write an application to the Chief Proctor
- Attach: ID Card copy + Last Fee Receipt
- Time: Usually issued within 24-48 hours from the Proctor Section

**Required Documents:**
- Application to Chief Proctor
- ID Card copy
- Last Fee Receipt

---

### 7. Student Representation & Reimbursement Protocol

**Use this when representing ECA Ajmer in:**
- Hackathons
- Sports (BTU Tournaments)
- Robowars
- Research Conferences
- Any competitive events

**The Golden Rule: Pre-Approval (Before You Go)**
- Never go to an event assuming you will get reimbursed later if you didn't get permission in writing first
- The college needs to "sanction" the budget head before the trip

**Step 1: The "Permission & OD" Application**

**Timing:** Submit this at least 7–10 days before the event

**To:** The Principal (Through HOD)

**Subject:** "Permission to participate in [Event Name] and Request for Financial Assistance & On-Duty (OD) Leave."

**Attachments Required:**
- Official Event Brochure/Invitation (showing date, venue, and registration fees)
- Acceptance Letter (if you were selected for a final round)
- Estimated Budget (Travel fare + Registration fee + Accommodation)

**Step 2: Routing the File**
- You → HOD (Remarks: "Recommended for student development")
- HOD → Proctor Section (for OD attendance verification)
- Proctor Section → Principal (for Approval)

**Pro Tip:** Ask the Principal to mark it to the "Account Section" or "Student Welfare Fund" / "TEQIP Cell" (if active) for funding.

**Step 3: Attendance - The "On Duty" (OD) Leave**

**What it is:** It marks your attendance as "Present" while you are away.

**Procedure:**
1. Keep the Photocopy of the approved permission letter (from Step 1)
2. After you return, write a "Joining Report" stating you have resumed classes
3. Attach the Certificate of Participation copy
4. Submit this bundle to your Department Office to update your attendance register

**Step 4: Reimbursement Process (Getting Money Back)**

**If you got Pre-Approval, here is how to claim the money:**

**Submit Within 7 Days:** Do not delay after returning.

**The "Reimbursement Application":**
- Reference your "Pre-Approval Letter" (Attach the copy)
- Subject: "Submission of Bills for [Event Name]."

**Required Evidences (The "Audit" Trap):**
1. Original Tickets: Bus/Train tickets (Boarding pass is mandatory for flights, though flights are rarely reimbursed for students)
2. Original Registration Receipt: The fee receipt from the event organizers
3. Certificate Copy: Proof you actually attended
4. Geotagged Photos: 1-2 photos of you at the venue (preferably with the banner)
5. Brief Report: A 1-page summary of what you learned/achieved

**Submission:**
- Submit to the Accounts Section (Admin Building)
- You will need to provide your Bank Account details (Passbook copy)

**Sample Application Template:**

```
To,
The Principal,
Engineering College Ajmer.
Through: HOD, [Your Dept]

Subject: Permission to represent college in [Event Name] and request for OD/Funding.

Respected Sir,

I, [Name], student of [Year/Branch], have been selected to represent ECA at [Name of Competition] to be held at [Venue] from [Date] to [Date]. This is a prestigious event organized by [Organizer, e.g., IIT Bombay/BTU].

I request you to kindly:
1. Grant me On-Duty (OD) leave from [Date] to [Date].
2. Sanction financial assistance of approx ₹[Amount] for Registration and Travel expenses (Budget attached).

I will submit all original bills and the participation certificate upon my return.

Yours Obediently,
[Name & Roll No]
[Mobile Number]
```

### Summary Checklist

| Document | Who Signs/Approves? | Key Requirement |
|----------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Medical Leave | HOD + Principal | Medical + Fitness Certificate |
| Internship NOC | HOD + TPO Cell | Company Offer Letter |
| No Dues | Library, Sports, Accounts, HOD | Original Caution Money Receipt |
| Bonafide | Chief Proctor | Current Fee Receipt |
| Student Representation | HOD + Proctor + Principal | Pre-approval, Event Brochure, Budget |
| Reimbursement | Accounts Section | Original Bills, Certificate, Photos, Report |

### Important Contact Information

**Proctor Section:** For OD, Bonafide, No Dues forms
**T&P Cell:** For Internship NOC
**Accounts Section:** For TC, Caution Money, Reimbursement
**Scholarship Section:** Usually in Proctor Office or Admin Block
**Chief Proctor:** For Bonafide/Character Certificate

**Location:** Main Building / Admin Building
