Student Activity Center As the name suggests, Student Activity Centre (SAC) is the hub of all student activities and hosts events like musical nights, stand-up comedy, talks, plays, and educational sessions. It's also a space for underprivileged children to receive education from volunteers. SAC includes a sports complex equipped with a modern gym, table tennis, carrom, chess, and outdoor grounds for volleyball, kabaddi, and kho-kho.
