It works under the guidance of the Training & Placement Officer in consultation with the Faculty coordinators and TPO assistant. T&P Cell helps final - year students who wish to find suitable jobs after the completion of their courses or programs. However, the training and g rooming commences from the very first year. T&P Cell's efforts have been characterized by the ability to identify talent, classify students, and address their strengths and shortcomings. T&P Cell provides full placement support to its students in all areas of disciplines - Computer Engineering, Information Technology, Cyber Security, Electronic and Communication Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and more.
