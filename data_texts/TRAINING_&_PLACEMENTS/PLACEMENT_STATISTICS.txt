### Placement Trends & Analysis

**Highest Placement Year:** 2021-2022 (274 placements, 73 companies)

**Top Performing Branches (Overall):**
1. Computer Science (CS) - Consistently highest placements
2. Information Technology (IT) - Strong performance
3. Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE) - Good placements
4. Mechanical Engineering (ME) - Strong in recent years
5. Electrical Engineering (EE) - Steady growth

**Growth Trends:**
- Placement numbers have shown significant growth from 2017-2018 (67) to 2021-2022 (274)
- Computer Science branch consistently leads in placements
- Core branches (Civil, EIC) have lower but improving placement numbers
- MCA and MBA placements are limited but present in recent years

**Company Visits:**
- Peak year: 2021-2022 (73 companies)
- Average companies per year: 30-40 companies
- Consistent company participation across years
