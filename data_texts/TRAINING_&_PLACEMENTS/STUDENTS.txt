ECA TPO Students Placement Guidelines The Training & Placement Cell of Engineering College Ajmer (ECA) is committed to ensuring a smooth and transparent placement process for students. All eligible students seeking placement assistance are required to adhere to the following guidelines: Only students who have registered for the placement process will be allowed to participate in campus drives. Students must attend all placement - related training, sessions, and workshops conducted by the T&P Cell. It is mandatory to maintain discipline and professionalism during interactions with company representatives. Students are advised to keep multiple updated copies of their resumes and required documents for all placement activities. Once a student receives a job offer, they will be considered out of the placement pool (unless it's a dream/above - package opportunity as per policy).
