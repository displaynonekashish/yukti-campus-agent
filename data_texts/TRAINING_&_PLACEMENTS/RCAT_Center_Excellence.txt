*** RCAT & CENTER OF EXCELLENCE (Robotics & Automation) ***

### 1. WHAT IS IT?
* **Full Name:** Rajiv Gandhi Centre of Advanced Technology (RCAT) / Center of Excellence.
* **Location:** Situated between the Old Building and New Building (at the location of the former Old Canteen).
* **Objective:** To provide advanced industrial training in Robotics, Cloud Computing, and Automation to make students industry-ready.

### 2. MANAGEMENT & FACULTY
* **Key Coordinator:** Dr. Dilip Sisodia (CS Dept) [and other associated faculty].
* **Department Involvement:** Primarily managed in collaboration with Computer Science and Mechanical departments for interdisciplinary projects.

### 3. COURSES & TRAINING
* **Technologies:**
  + Robotics & Industrial Automation
  + Augmented Reality (AR) / Virtual Reality (VR)
  + Auto-CAD & Manufacturing simulation
* **Certification:** Courses usually offer certificates valid for industrial training credits.

### 4. HOW TO REGISTER
* **Online:** Visit the official RCAT or College website for course listings.
* **Offline (In-Campus):** Students can visit the RCAT Center (Between Old & New Building) directly to inquire about upcoming batches.
* **External Students:** Registration is often open to students from other colleges as well, subject to batch availability.
