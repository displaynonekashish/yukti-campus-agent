ECA TPO Internship Internship Policy Engineering College, Ajmer (ECA) emphasizes the importance of internships as a vital component of professional development. The internship policy aims to ensure that students gain real - world industry exposure that complements their academic learning. As per AICTE and university guidelines, internships are mandatory for all undergraduate students and encouraged for postgraduate students to enhance practical skills, technical understanding, and workplace readiness. The policy allows students to pursue internships during semester breaks, summer vacations, or as a part of credit - based curriculum components. These internships may be full - time or part - time, on - site or virtual, and must be aligned with the student's area of study. All internships must be approved by the Training & Placement Cell in coordination with respective departments to ensure quality and relevance. Process For Approval To maintain academic standards and ensure the relevance of internships, students must follow the structured process laid out by the Training & Placement Cell: 
1. Proposal Sub mission: Students must submit a formal application including details of the company, duration, domain of work, and internship offer letter or confirmation email. Department Review: The application is forwarded to the respective Head of Department (HOD) for verification of academic relevance and feasibility. 
3. Approval by T&P Cell: Upon departmental clearance, the T&P Cell issues formal approval along with a No Objection Certificate (NOC) if required by the company. 
4. Completion and Reporting: After completing the internship, students must submit a detailed internship report, feedback from the company/mentor, and present their learning outcomes to an internal evaluation panel.
