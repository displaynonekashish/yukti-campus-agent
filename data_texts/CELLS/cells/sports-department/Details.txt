Sports Department Details Circulars Members Details The Sports Department of the college aims to promote physical fitness, sportsmanship, and overall well-being among students. • It provides facilities and opportunities for students to participate in various indoor and outdoor sports activities, encouraging teamwork, discipline, and healthy competition. • The department organizes training, coaching, and inter-college competitions to help students develop their skills and achieve excellence in sports. • It also ensures proper maintenance of sports infrastructure and equipment to provide a safe and conducive environment for sports activities. • Sports Facilities Include: One indoor sports complex with modern gym equipment and facilities for Table-Tennis, Carrom, Chess. • One outdoor ground for playing Volleyball, Kabaddi, Kho-Kho, and other outdoor sports.
