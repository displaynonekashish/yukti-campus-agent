Industry Institute Interaction Cell (IIIC) Details Circulars Members Details The Industry Institute Interaction Cell (IIIC) at Government Engineering College Ajmer serves as a vital bridge between academia and industry. • Through this cell, significant progress has been achieved in fostering collaboration, innovation, and mutual growth. • Each academic department actively participates in IIIC initiatives to strengthen ties with industries and align academic learning with real-world applications. • In today’s rapidly evolving technological world, strong interaction between institutes and industries has become essential. • For students, it provides practical exposure, skill enhancement, and better placement opportunities. • For industries, it ensures access to a skilled, industry-ready workforce capable of adapting to global competition. • Recognizing this mutual need, the Industry Institute Interaction Cell was established to: Facilitate continuous communication between students, ### Faculty , and industries. • Prepare students for professional excellence in line with current industry trends. • Encourage industries to collaborate with academia for ### Research and development initiatives. • Major Focus of IIIC To design and evolve educational programs aligned with industry needs and emerging technologies. • To provide opportunities for engineers and technologists to upgrade their knowledge and stay industry-relevant. • To coordinate and promote joint ### Research and development activities between academia and industry. • Functions of IIIC Organizing workshops, seminars, and expert lectures with joint participation of ### Faculty and industry professionals. • Establishing Mo Us (Memoranda of Understanding) between the institute and industries for strategic collaboration. • Offering professional consultancy by ### Faculty members to industries. • Facilitating industrial visits, student internships, and B. • E. • projects in partnership with industry mentors. • Creating an Innovation Portal to showcase projects developed by students and ### Faculty . • Providing industrial training and integrating real-world exposure into the teaching-learning process.
