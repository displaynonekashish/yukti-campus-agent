Institution's Innovation Cell (IIC) Details Circulars Members Details The Ministry of Human Resource Development (MHRD), Government of India, established the MHRD’s Innovation Cell (MIC) with the objective of fostering a culture of innovation across all Higher Education Institutions (HEIs). The primary goal of MIC is to encourage, inspire, and nurture young students to work on innovative ideas and transform them into impactful solutions during their formative years. To achieve this vision, Institution’s Innovation Councils (IICs) have been created across selected HEIs. These councils form a nationwide network that promotes innovation through various structured activities and initiatives, building a vibrant innovation ecosystem within campuses. For more information about IICs and current calendar activities, please visit: 🔗 www. mic. gov. in/iic. html Major Focus of IIC To create a vibrant local innovation ecosystem within the institution. To establish startup and entrepreneurship support mechanisms for students and faculty. To prepare the institute for the Atal Ranking of Institutions on Innovation Achievements (ARIIA) framework. To develop a functional ecosystem for scouting and pre-incubation of innovative ideas. To enhance cognitive and creative abilities among students of technology and management disciplines. Functions of IIC Conduct various innovation and entrepreneurship-related activities as prescribed by the Central MIC within a time-bound framework. Identify and reward innovative ideas, and share success stories within and outside the campus. Organize workshops, seminars, and interactions with entrepreneurs, investors, and industry professionals; create a strong mentor pool for student innovators. Build networks with peer institutions and national entrepreneurship development organizations. Develop an Institutional Innovation Portal to showcase innovative projects by students and faculty. Organize hackathons, idea competitions, and innovation challenges with active involvement from industries. Benefits of IIC No major capital investment is required, as the council leverages the existing local ecosystem. Students and faculty get exclusive opportunities to participate in national-level innovation and startup initiatives organized by the MHRD. Access to certifications, prizes, and recognition for innovation achievements. Direct interaction with renowned business leaders, entrepreneurs, and academicians. Opportunities to prototype, experiment, and validate new ideas under expert mentorship. Hands-on exposure to latest technologies and entrepreneurial practices. Opportunities for travel, networking, and cultural learning through IIC events.
