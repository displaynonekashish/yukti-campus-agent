and eco-friendly initiatives within the campus and surrounding communities. • The Cell acts as a catalyst for integrating environmental consciousness into academic, administrative, and social strives to nurture a culture of responsibility towards nature among students and staff. • The Environment Cell also collaborates with government bodies, NGOs, and local organizations to implement
