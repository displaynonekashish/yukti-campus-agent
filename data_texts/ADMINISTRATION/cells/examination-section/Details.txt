Examination Section Details Circulars Members Details The Examination Section of Engineering College, Ajmer plays a vital role in managing all examination-related activities in close coordination with Bikaner Technical University (BTU), Bikaner. The section’s key responsibilities include the online sub mission of examination forms through the BTU portal, distribution of admit cards to students, conduct of examinations at the college center, uploading of internal and practical marks by faculty members, and systematic analysis of results after declaration. It also facilitates the collection and distribution of mark sheets, pro visional certificates, and degrees to students in coordination with the university. Supported by a dedicated team of faculty members and non-teaching staff, the Examination Section is committed to maintaining the highest standards of fairness, confidentiality, and transparency in all examination-related operations. Objective: To conduct fair, transparent, and efficient examination processes that ensures academic integrity while maintaining the highest standards. Vision: To be a leader in conducting high-quality examinations that foster academic excellence and subsequently contributing to the development of skilled engineers who serve society. Mission: To implement examination systems that promotes academic integrity and provide a seamless experience for students, faculty, and staff. To maintain confidentiality, security, and transparency in all examination-related activities. To continuously evaluate and improve examination processes, leveraging technology and best practices to enhance efficiency and effectiveness. To provide tabulated results to the faculty to enable them to analyse results so that corrective measures can be taken to improve performance of students.
