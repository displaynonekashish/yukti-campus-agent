Account Section Details Circulars Members Details The Accounts Section of Engineering College, Ajmer is responsible for managing all financial transactions and accounting operations of the institution with transparency, efficiency, and strict adherence to government financial norms. The section ensures the proper maintenance of financial records, preparation of budgets, and effective utilization of funds for academic, administrative, and developmental activities. Its key functions include the preparation and processing of salary bills, management of student fee records, collection of semester and examination fees through both online and offline modes, payment of bills, and maintenance of ledgers and audit documents. The section also coordinates with Bikaner Technical University (BTU), Bikaner, and other concerned authorities for the sub mission of financial reports and compliance-related matters. Supported by a dedicated team of officers and staff, the Accounts Section strives to uphold accuracy, accountability, and integrity in all financial dealings, thereby ensuring the smooth and transparent financial management of the institution. Vision To establish a transparent, efficient, and accountable financial management system that ensures optimal utilization of resources and supports the institution’s academic and administrative excellence. Mission To maintain accurate and up-to-date financial records in accordance with government norms and accounting standards. To ensure timely processing of salaries, payments, and reimbursements. To manage the collection of student tuition and examination fees through secure online and offline systems. To prepare and monitor annual budgets and expenditure plans effectively. To facilitate financial audits and ensure compliance with BTU and government requirements. To promote transparency, accountability, and fiscal discipline in all financial operations. Objectives To record and manage all financial transactions systematically and securely. To ensure timely disbursement of salaries, payments, and reimbursements. To collect and maintain accurate records of student semester and examination fees. To prepare annual budgets, financial statements, and audit reports. To maintain coordination with Bikaner Technical University (BTU) and other regulatory agencies. To monitor the utilization of funds for various institutional activities. To promote the use of digital tools for efficient financial management and reporting. To uphold transparency, reliability, and integrity in all financial matters.
